Piano Frequency Reference

Complete frequency chart and acoustic data for all 88 keys, calibrated to the international A440 (ISO 16) tuning standard.

Acoustic Calibration & Standards

Modern keyboard acoustics are derived using Twelve-Tone Equal Temperament: f(n) = 440 × 2^((n-69)/12). For practical instruction on tuning instruments by ear to standard pitch, explore the A440 reference guide. For treble register note calculations and harmonic profiles, see the G5 frequency reference.
